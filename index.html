Support & Help

Kardoo remembers what you know and brings each word back right before you’d forget it. Here’s how to get the most out of it, and how to reach us.

Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

Every plan starts with 3 days free for new subscribers, with one free trial per Apple Account across all Kardoo plans. You pay nothing on the day you start. If you don’t cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription begins and your Apple Account is charged for the plan you chose. The App Store shows the date and the price in your currency before you confirm.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

In Kardoo, open Settings → Subscription and tap Manage Subscription to cancel, or Change Plan to switch between Monthly, Yearly and Family. You can also cancel anytime in the iPhone Settings app (tap your name → Subscriptions) or on the App Store subscriptions page. After you cancel, you keep full access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

I already have a subscription. How do I get access back?

Make sure your iPhone is signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe. Then tap I Already Have a Subscription on the welcome screen, or Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. If someone in your family has Kardoo Family, you need to be in their Family Sharing group.

How do I get a refund?

Payments for Kardoo go through Apple, so Apple handles refunds, not us. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, and Apple decides under its own terms.

Can my family use one subscription?

Yes, with Kardoo Family. Through Apple’s Family Sharing it covers you and up to 5 family members, so up to 6 people. Kardoo Monthly and Kardoo Yearly are for one person.

Which languages can I learn?

English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Russian, Ukrainian, Portuguese, Dutch, Swedish, Czech, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Polish, plus any other language you add with a name of your own. Kardoo itself speaks English, Polish and Spanish. Switch in Settings → App → App Language.

How do I add words from a photo, a file or a list?

When you add words, choose Paste List, Photo or File. Take a photo of a notebook or textbook page (the camera straightens the page), or pick a picture from your library. Kardoo recognizes the text on your iPhone. Files can be CSV, TSV, TXT, PDF or images. Every import ends on Review Words, where you can fix, untick or tag words before you save them.

Why are some translations missing?

Kardoo fills empty translations with Apple’s built-in translator, which needs its language files on your iPhone. Allow the download when iOS asks. If the Translate app is installed, you can see downloaded languages in Settings → Apps → Translate → Downloaded Languages. Apple doesn’t support every language pair yet, and you can always type a translation yourself.

How does Kardoo decide when to show a word again?

Kardoo uses spaced repetition, based on the SM-2 method. A word you know comes back after 1 day, then after 6 days, then at longer and longer intervals. A word you miss comes back sooner. The stars on each word show how well you know it, and you can change them yourself.

Do my words sync and survive a reinstall?

Yes, as long as iCloud Backup is on (Settings → Data; it’s on by default). Your words, progress and settings are kept in your own iCloud, so they come back when you reinstall Kardoo or set up a new iPhone with the same Apple Account.

How can I export my words?

Settings → Data → CSV Export creates a file with all your words. It opens in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets, and you choose where to send or save it.

How do I delete my data?

Inside Kardoo you can delete single words, or a whole language with its words and history (touch and hold the language, then Delete Language). Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. To remove the copy in iCloud too, open the iPhone Settings app → your name → iCloud → Storage (on some versions, Manage Account Storage) → Kardoo, and delete its data. Turning iCloud Backup off in Kardoo only stops syncing. It doesn’t delete what is already in iCloud.

Does Kardoo collect my data?

No. There’s no Kardoo account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your words stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Plans

Kardoo needs an active subscription. Every plan gives the same full access to the app:

  • Kardoo Monthly renews every month.
  • Kardoo Yearly renews every year.
  • Kardoo Family renews every year and can be shared with up to 5 family members through Family Sharing.

Free trial

New subscribers get 3 days free, with one free trial per Apple Account across all Kardoo plans. If you don’t cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription begins and you’re charged. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.

Payment and automatic renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase or, with a free trial, when the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends. If the price changes, Apple tells you in advance and asks for your consent where the law requires it.

Cancelling and changing plans

You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime: in Kardoo (Settings → Subscription), or in your Apple Account settings (iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions). After you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for. Without an active subscription Kardoo stays locked, but your words aren’t deleted. They stay on your iPhone and in your iCloud and come back when you subscribe again.

Requirements

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Syncing needs an iCloud account with free iCloud storage. Scanning a page needs the iPhone camera, and Apple’s translator may need to download language files first.

Your content

The words, lists and photos you add are yours. Kardoo keeps them only on your iPhone and in your iCloud. Please add only content you’re allowed to use. Text recognition and automatic translations can contain mistakes, so check them on the Review Words screen before you save.
