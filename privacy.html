Privacy Policy

Kardoo doesn’t track you and doesn’t show ads. This page explains exactly what happens to your data in the app and on this website.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

At a glance

  • No Kardoo account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Kardoo contains no third-party code.
  • Your words, progress and settings stay on your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud when sync is on. We can’t see them.
  • Text in photos and files is recognized on your iPhone, and Kardoo doesn’t save the photos.
  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account.
  • On the App Store, Kardoo is labeled “Data Not Collected”.

Who is responsible

Kardoo is published by Ignacy Myczkowski, a private individual based in Poland (“we”, “us”). For the personal data we process ourselves, which is only the emails you send us, Ignacy Myczkowski is the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can reach us at ignacy.myczkowski@icloud.com.

Data stored on your iPhone

Kardoo keeps what you put into it in the app’s private storage on your iPhone:

  • Your languages and words: each word with its translation, part of speech, tags and star rating.
  • Your learning history: when you practiced each word, whether your answer was right, which mode you used, and when the word is due again.
  • Your settings: daily goal, review order, theme, accent color, glass effect and app language.
  • Your first name, if you enter it on the welcome screens or in Settings. It’s optional, up to 24 characters, and only used to greet you.

None of this is sent to us. Kardoo may also keep a one-time safety copy of its database, made on your iPhone before iCloud sync was introduced. It never leaves your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app.

iCloud sync

iCloud Backup is on by default, and you can turn it off anytime in Settings → Data → iCloud Backup. While it’s on:

  • your languages, words and learning history are stored in Kardoo’s private database in your iCloud account, using Apple’s CloudKit;
  • your settings and your first name are stored in your iCloud key-value storage, so they come back after a reinstall.

This data belongs to your Apple Account. As the developer, we have no access to your private iCloud data and can’t read, change or delete it. Apple stores and protects it under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Turning iCloud Backup off stops syncing, but it doesn’t delete what is already in iCloud. To delete that too, open the iPhone Settings app → your name → iCloud → Storage (on some versions, Manage Account Storage) → Kardoo, and delete its data.

Camera, photos and files

Kardoo asks for camera access only when you choose to take a photo of a page. The camera is used only for Apple’s document scanner, and nothing is recorded in the background.

When you pick a picture from your library, iOS shows its own photo picker, and Kardoo receives only the photo you chose. It can’t see the rest of your library. Files are opened only when you pick them.

Text in photos, PDFs and files is recognized on your iPhone with Apple’s Vision technology. Kardoo doesn’t save the photos or files themselves. It keeps only the words you confirm on the Review Words screen.

Translation and language detection

When an imported word has no translation, Kardoo asks Apple’s built-in translator (the iOS Translation framework) to fill it in. Kardoo also uses Apple’s language detection to work out which language a list is in. The translator uses language files that iOS downloads to your iPhone after asking you.

These features are part of iOS and covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. Kardoo doesn’t send your words to us or to any other company.

Exporting your words

CSV Export creates the file on your iPhone and opens the iOS share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold through the App Store, and Apple processes the payment. We never see your payment details, your name, your email address or your Apple Account. On your iPhone, Kardoo asks Apple’s StoreKit whether you have an active subscription, which plan it is and whether you can still start a free trial.

Apple gives developers sales and subscription reports that don’t show who you are, for example the number of subscriptions in a country. When you download Kardoo or browse the App Store, Apple processes data under its own privacy policy.

Crash reports from Apple

If you allow it on your iPhone (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers), Apple shares crash reports and app usage statistics with developers. We see them only in Apple’s developer tools, in aggregated or de-identified form, and we use them only to find and fix problems. You can switch this off anytime in the same place.

When you email us

When you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and whatever you write, including any attachments. We use this only to answer you and help with your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in replying (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or steps you asked us to take (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Emails are kept in our iCloud Mail mailbox, provided by Apple. We keep them for up to 2 years after your matter is closed, unless we need them longer to establish or defend legal claims. We never use them for marketing.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open it, GitHub’s servers process your IP address and standard technical data, such as your browser type and the time of the request, to deliver the page and keep the service secure. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

GitHub transfers this data from the EU to the USA under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

This website sets no cookies, has no analytics or trackers, and loads no fonts or scripts from other companies.

What we don’t collect

  • No account, email address, phone number or postal address, unless you email us.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or health data.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier and no tracking across apps and websites.
  • No analytics or tracking tools. Kardoo contains no third-party code.
  • We don’t sell or share any data.

That’s why Kardoo’s App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability.

Your Kardoo data is on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, so you can do most of this yourself. You can edit or delete words and languages, export everything with CSV Export, turn iCloud Backup off, or delete the app. For emails you’ve sent us, write to us and we’ll respond within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Poland, that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl). You can also go to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

Children

Kardoo is a learning app for people of any age, and we don’t collect personal data from anyone, including children. Purchases go through Apple, so parents can control them with Ask to Buy and Screen Time. The optional first name stays on the device and in that person’s own iCloud.

Security

Your data is protected by iOS app sandboxing and by the security of your Apple Account and iCloud. Kardoo has no servers of its own, so there is no Kardoo database that could leak.

Changes to this policy

If Kardoo starts handling data differently, we’ll update this policy before the change reaches the app and revise the date at the top of this page.
